Project standard

What every AVERIQ project includes

Regardless of category, project work is documented to the same standard.

1

Stated objective

What the project set out to establish, and what would have counted as a negative result.

2

Documented methodology

The method, the baselines it is compared against, and why those baselines are the right ones.

3

Working implementation

Source code with environment files, seeds, configurations and run instructions.

4

Experimental record

Real experimental runs, logged results and the evaluation procedure used to produce them.

5

Honest outcome

What the results support, what they do not, and the limitations a reviewer will ask about.

We do not publish client project details, performance figures or institutional names without written permission, and we do not display statistics, testimonials or partnerships we cannot evidence.
