Research and Publication Support

For researchers, faculty members, PhD scholars, postgraduate students and research institutions.

Services

Support across the full research lifecycle

Every engagement is work done with you. Scope is agreed in writing before anything starts, and each deliverable comes with an explanation session — because you have to be able to defend it.

Research Foundations

Research Consultation
A working session on where your research currently stands: what the question really is, whether the scope fits your timeline, and which direction is worth committing to. You leave with a written summary of the options and the trade-offs between them.
Topic Selection
Narrowing a broad interest into a specific, feasible research problem — one that has an open gap, available data, and a realistic path to results within the time you have. We check feasibility against your hardware and dataset access before recommending anything.
Systematic Literature Review
A structured sweep of the field: search strategy, inclusion criteria, and a comparison table of what existing work actually does, what it assumes, and where it stops. Delivered as an annotated reading list you can cite from directly.
Research Gap Analysis
Identifying a gap you can defend — not a gap that merely has not been written about. We separate genuine methodological openings from topics nobody pursued because they do not work, and state the evidence behind the distinction.
Research Proposal
Problem statement, objectives, methodology, expected outcomes and timeline, structured to your institution's format. Built on your own idea and refined with you, so you can present and defend every section of it.

Methodology & Implementation

Methodology & Experimental Design
Study design decided before anything is run: baselines, data splits, evaluation protocol, ablation plan and what would count as a negative result. This is the step that prevents experiments you have to repeat later.
Algorithm Implementation
Working implementations of algorithms, mathematical models and complete experimental pipelines, written for your problem rather than adapted from an unrelated repository. Delivered with environment files, seeds and run instructions.
Deep Learning Model Development
Architecture design and training pipelines in PyTorch or TensorFlow — data loaders, loss functions, schedulers, checkpointing and logging. Sized to the GPU you actually have, with memory and runtime accounted for.
Dataset Preparation
Acquisition, cleaning, annotation handling, preprocessing and splitting — built so that no information leaks between training and test folds, which is the single most common reason reviewers reject reported numbers.
Experiment Pipelines
Configuration-driven pipelines that run a full experimental grid end to end, log every run, and produce the result tables directly from the logs rather than by hand. Re-runnable months later with identical output.

Experiments & Validation

Baseline Reproduction
Re-implementing the methods you need to compare against, and getting their reported numbers to reproduce. Where they do not reproduce, we document precisely why — which is itself a defensible contribution.
Reproducibility Audit
Tracing the gap between a paper and its repository: undocumented preprocessing, mismatched splits, different evaluation protocols, or results that depend on an unstated choice. Delivered as a written audit with evidence for each finding.
Ablation & Benchmarking
The ablation grid reviewers will ask for — each component removed in turn, under identical conditions, with the comparison held fair. Includes the benchmark harness so you can extend it yourself.
Statistical Analysis
Appropriate tests for your design, effect sizes, confidence intervals and correction for multiple comparisons. We say plainly when a difference is not statistically meaningful, even when it would read better if it were.
Results Interpretation
What the numbers support, what they do not, and which limitations an examiner or reviewer will raise first. Written as analysis you can use in the discussion section, not as a restatement of the table.

Writing & Documentation

Manuscript Structuring
Section-by-section structure and argument: what each section must establish, in what order, and which claims need evidence attached. Worked through with you so the argument is yours and you can defend it.
Scientific Language Editing
Clarity, precision and academic register — tightening overlong sentences, fixing hedging that weakens real findings, and removing claims the results do not support. Your voice and your argument are preserved.
LaTeX & Formatting
Camera-ready setup for IEEE, ACM, Springer, Elsevier and university thesis templates — bibliography management, cross-referencing, table and figure placement, and the compile errors that appear the night before a deadline.
Figures & Scientific Visualization
Publication-grade figures generated from your actual results: architecture diagrams, qualitative comparisons, plots with proper axes, units and error bars, exported at the resolution and format the venue requires.
Thesis & Dissertation Support
Chapter-by-chapter development and review for M.Tech, MS and PhD theses, plus compilation, consistency checks across chapters, and viva preparation on the questions your committee is most likely to ask.

Publication Support

Journal & Venue Selection
Matching your work to venues by scope, methodological fit and realistic review timeline. We flag predatory and low-quality venues explicitly and will not recommend them, whatever the acceptance rate.
Submission Preparation
The full submission package — formatting compliance, author guidelines, cover letter, supplementary material, anonymisation for double-blind review, and a final pre-submission check against the reviewer rubric.
Reviewer Response Support
Reading reviewer comments accurately, separating what must change from what needs a better explanation, and drafting a point-by-point response that addresses the substance rather than deflecting it.
Review & Survey Papers
Taxonomy design, systematic coverage, comparison tables and the synthesis that makes a survey worth citing — structured so it contributes an argument about the field rather than listing papers.
Book Chapter Preparation
Chapter scoping, structure and development for edited volumes, with the pedagogical framing and self-contained background that book chapters require but journal papers do not.

The AVERIQ commitment

Your Research. Genuine Code. Authentic Results.

We believe quality research must be supported by genuine implementation and scientifically valid results. We do not produce superficial research outputs, copied implementations, fabricated experimental results, or misleading performance claims. Every implementation is developed with attention to methodological correctness, technical accuracy, experimental validation and reproducibility.

Genuine source code

Actual working code written for your research problem, methodology, dataset and experimental requirements.

Authentic results

Outcomes emerge from real computational experiments and valid evaluation procedures — never fabricated metrics.

Research integrity

Documented code, configurations and evaluation procedures, so the work can be independently verified and reproduced.

Continuous support

Assistance does not end at delivery. We stay with the work through the final research and publication stages.
